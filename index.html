How to read it. Badges show where each line came from: RMA or FRA means the shop report said it, and general means standard Front Range timing filled the gap. The reports are weekly snapshots, so months other than the current one lean on general timing. Check the hatch chart's legend to see which is which.

Drive times are estimates from Gunbarrel without traffic, not routed. Pins sit at the access point named in the report; open each one in Google Maps for directions. Basemaps: USGS The National Map (topo and satellite) and OpenTopoMap (terrain). Check CPW and National Park Service regulations before you fish.
